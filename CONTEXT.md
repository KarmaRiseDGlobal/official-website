# KarmaRise D Global

KarmaRise D Global connects product businesses with Indian manufacturing and stays involved from the Product Brief through Bulk Order delivery. Almarise and the planned Women's Training Initiative belong to the wider company story but are not the current website's primary offer.

## Manufacturing

**Client**:
A European retail chain, independent retailer, or fashion brand that commissions a bulk product.
_Avoid_: Customer, shopowner, buyer

**Manufacturing Programme**:
An end-to-end engagement in which KarmaRise D Global develops and delivers a client's bulk product through Indian manufacturing.
_Avoid_: Deal, brokerage, sourcing job

**Product Brief**:
The agreed description of the product a client needs, including its design, materials, quantity, quality, and delivery expectations.
_Avoid_: Idea, request, requirements list

**Manufacturing Partner**:
An Indian factory selected to produce a product under a Manufacturing Programme.
_Avoid_: Vendor, supplier

**Bulk Order**:
The production quantity commissioned by a client under a Manufacturing Programme.
_Avoid_: Purchase, shipment

## Company and brands

**KarmaRise D Global**:
The parent brand and the provider of Manufacturing Programmes.
_Avoid_: Karmarise D Global, Karmarise Global, Kamarise, Karma Rise D Global

**Almarise**:
A fashion brand owned by KarmaRise D Global that will transform existing Maharashtrian sarees into contemporary women's clothing.
_Avoid_: Manufacturing service, NGO, separate company

**Women's Training Initiative**:
A planned programme to train women in Maharashtra in garment design and stitching, with employment opportunities as a long-term aim.
_Avoid_: NGO, charity, operating programme

## Publishing

**Story**:
An editorial account of manufacturing, materials, people, completed work, or company progress published by KarmaRise D Global.
_Avoid_: Blog post, news item
