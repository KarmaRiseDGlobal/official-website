## 2. Mandatory non-fabricated claims and confidentiality guardrails

Attach this clause verbatim to every creator brief, gifting card, and sponsorship agreement.

### English guardrails (attach to EN briefs)

1. **No pricing, margins, or MOQs:** Never state unit prices, agency fees, margins, or fixed minimum order quantities. Every KarmaRise D Global Manufacturing Programme is scoped individually per Product Brief.
2. **Strict confidentiality:** Never name confidential manufacturing Clients (including `Snoby`) and never mention `Ulhasnagar` as a production or office town. Describe our Indian base as `"our studio in India"`, `"near Mumbai"`, or `"Maharashtra"`, alongside specialist craft hubs in Kolkata, Kanpur, Agra, and Chennai.
3. **Accurate Women's Training Initiative status:** If mentioned, state plainly that the **Women's Training Initiative** is a planned programme currently in preparation and that applications have not opened yet. Never claim it is already enrolling students and never use charity or pity framing.
4. **Exact protected brand names:** Always write and say **`KarmaRise D Global`**, **`AlmaRise D Brand`**, **`AlmaRise D Reborn`**, **`AlmaRise D Organic`**, and **`AlmaRise D Home Decor`**. Never shorten the fashion label to standalone `"AlmaRise"` or `"Almarise"`.
5. **Respectful framing of `thevnitlya sadya`:** Describe `AlmaRise D Reborn` fabrics accurately as treasured sarees kept for years by Indian women that carry a real family story, never as cheap textile scrap or waste rags.
6. **Accurate product scope and geography:** KarmaRise D Global manufactures four core categories: leather goods, clothing, textile accessories, and textile home decor. Always say `"Spain and the rest of Europe"` (Spain is part of Europe).

### Reglas obligatorias en español (adjuntar a briefings ES)

1. **Sin precios públicos ni mínimos cerrados:** No menciones tarifas unitarias, comisiones, márgenes ni cantidades mínimas fijas. Cada Programa de producción de KarmaRise D Global se estudia a medida según el briefing de producto.
2. **Confidencialidad estricta:** No menciones clientes confidenciales (incluido `Snoby`) ni cites `Ulhasnagar`. Describe nuestra base en la India como `"nuestro estudio en la India"`, `"cerca de Bombay"` o `"Maharashtra"`, junto a centros especialistas en Calcuta, Kanpur, Agra y Chennai.
3. **Estado real de la Iniciativa de formación para mujeres:** Si se menciona, indica con claridad que está en preparación y que el plazo de inscripción aún no está abierto. Nunca la presentes como en funcionamiento ni uses lenguaje de caridad o lástima.
4. **Nombres de marca exactos:** Utiliza siempre **`KarmaRise D Global`**, **`AlmaRise D Brand`**, **`AlmaRise D Reborn`**, **`AlmaRise D Organic`** y **`AlmaRise D Home Decor`**. No acortes la marca a `"AlmaRise"` a secas.
5. **Respeto a los saris `thevnitlya sadya`:** Describe las piezas de `AlmaRise D Reborn` como prendas únicas creadas a partir de saris guardados con historia por mujeres en la India, nunca como retales baratos ni desechos textiles.
6. **Alcance y geografía precisos:** KarmaRise D Global fabrica cuatro categorías: bolsos y artículos de piel, ropa, complementos textiles y decoración textil del hogar. Di siempre `"España y el resto de Europa"`.

## 3. Track B: 1-page AlmaRise D Brand nano and micro gifting brief (Madrid street-test model)

Scale Alma's Madrid wear-test model (`Issue #02`): start by vetting 15 to 25 nano wearers (1k to 10k followers) in Spain and the rest of Europe who already wear natural fabrics and have genuine comment conversations. Send one finished `AlmaRise D Reborn` shrug (or `AlmaRise D Organic` / `AlmaRise D Home Decor` hero piece) with a handwritten card from Alma explaining the garment's cloth origin. Graduate the top 3 to 5 authentic organic wearers into paid micro collaborations (10k to 50k followers) with written usage rights.

### English 1-page gifting brief

- **Model:** Unpaid product gifting (zero obligation to post). If you genuinely enjoy wearing the piece and choose to share it, follow the disclosure and accuracy notes below.
- **Why this piece exists:** `AlmaRise D Reborn` turns treasured Indian sarees (`thevnitlya sadya`) kept in family cupboards into versatile everyday garments that revive outfits already hanging in your wardrobe. `AlmaRise D Organic` pairs organic dyes with fabrics made from sugarcane, orange, banana, and pineapple plant waste, among others, alongside `AlmaRise D Home Decor`.
- **Two to three talking points (in your own words, never scripted):**
  1. How the natural cloth feels and moves in everyday light and daily wear.
  2. How you styled it over items you already own (jeans, black basics, workwear, or evening wear).
  3. The real craft link between our India studio and everyday wardrobes in Spain and the rest of Europe.
- **Visual direction:** Natural daylight on the street, commute, or home interior. Avoid studio sales pitches.
- **Tag and link:** Tag `@almarisedbrand` on Instagram and link to `https://karmarisedglobal.com/for/<your-slug>` (or `https://karmarisedglobal.com/almarise?ref=<your-slug>`).
- **Mandatory disclosure:** Start your caption or Story overlay with `Ad · Gifted by AlmaRise D Brand` (UK) or `Gifted by AlmaRise D Brand` (US/international) before `"more..."`.

### Briefing de 1 página en español (envío de prenda / seeding)

- **Modalidad:** Regalo de producto sin obligación de publicar. Si te sientes cómoda con la prenda y te apetece compartirla, te pedimos respetar las notas de transparencia y rigor siguientes.
- **Por qué existe esta pieza:** `AlmaRise D Reborn` da una segunda vida a saris guardados con historia (`thevnitlya sadya`) convirtiéndolos en prendas de uso diario pensadas para renovar tu propio armario. `AlmaRise D Organic` trabaja con tintes orgánicos y tejidos de residuos de caña de azúcar, naranja, plátano y piña, entre otros, junto a `AlmaRise D Home Decor`.
- **Dos o tres ideas clave (siempre con tu propia voz, sin guion):**
  1. Cómo cae y se siente el tejido natural al llevarlo en movimiento.
  2. Cómo lo combinas con prendas que ya tienes (vaqueros, básicos negros, oficina o cena).
  3. El vínculo real entre nuestro estudio en la India y el día a día en España y el resto de Europa.
- **Enfoque visual:** Luz natural en la calle, en tu rutina o en casa. Sin tono comercial ni teletienda.
- **Etiqueta y enlace:** Etiqueta a `@almarisedbrand` en Instagram y enlaza a `https://karmarisedglobal.com/es/para/<tu-slug>` (o `https://karmarisedglobal.com/es/almarise?ref=<tu-slug>`).
- **Indicación legal obligatoria (España):** Coloca al principio del texto (antes de `"más..."`) y sobre la imagen o vídeo `Regalo de AlmaRise D Brand` o `Publicidad`. No uses solo `#collab` ni `#gracias`.

### Written usage rights and paid-ad whitelisting clause (3 to 6 months)

Never run a creator's organic or gifted post as a paid advertisement without signed permission. Include this addendum in every paid micro collaboration (and request signed consent before boosting any gifted post):

- **Organic repost license (EN):** Creator grants Karmarise Global LLP (`KarmaRise D Global` / `AlmaRise D Brand`) a non-exclusive, worldwide right for **6 months** from publication to repost the agreed photos, Reels, or Stories on `@almarisedbrand`, `@karmarisedglobal`, `karmarisedglobal.com`, and KarmaRise Stories newsletters with creator credit.
- **Paid-ad whitelisting / creator ads license (EN):** For the agreed flat fee plus usage addendum, Creator grants Karmarise Global LLP paid social usage and whitelisting (Meta Partnership Ads / spark authorization) rights for **[3 or 6] months** from first ad delivery, capped strictly to the approved deliverables, with one review round prior to launch and no transfer of underlying moral rights.
- **Cláusula de cesión de uso y whitelisting (ES):** La creadora concede a Karmarise Global LLP (`KarmaRise D Global` / `AlmaRise D Brand`) una licencia no exclusiva durante **6 meses** desde la publicación para compartir el contenido acordado de forma orgánica en `@almarisedbrand`, `@karmarisedglobal`, `karmarisedglobal.com` y las cartas de Historias de KarmaRise citando su autoría. En colaboraciones remuneradas con anexo de publicidad de pago, la creadora autoriza el uso en anuncios pagados y whitelisting (Meta Partnership Ads) durante **[3 o 6] meses** desde la activación, limitado a las piezas aprobadas tras una ronda de revisión.

## 4. Track A: B2B podcast and newsletter sponsorship insertion-order checklist

Target Spanish and European retail, independent boutique, leather-craft, and fashion-entrepreneurship podcasts and B2B newsletters whose listeners match Alma's ICP.

### Pre-buy vetting checklist (complete before booking)

- [ ] **Read 3 recent issues or listen to 2 episodes:** Confirm the audience includes independent shop owners, brand founders, or merchandising leads in Spain or the rest of Europe.
- [ ] **Verify median clicks, not subscriber count or open rates:** Open rates are inflated by Apple Mail Privacy Protection (MPP). Request median human clicks per sponsor slot across the last 5 sends.
- [ ] **Check sponsor renewals:** Ask how many recent sponsors renewed for a second or third slot and request one reference if spending above test budget.
- [ ] **Apply a 10% to 20% bot-click discount:** Corporate email security scanners follow newsletter links automatically. Divide flat fee by `(median clicks * 0.85)` to calculate true human CPC.
- [ ] **Test one send first:** Never sign a multi-issue bundle until one single placement proves real click and enquiry quality.
- [ ] **Check 30-day angle rotation:** Confirm no overlapping angle ran on the same list within the past 30 days.

### Insertion-order (IO) terms checklist

1. **Single file and single tracked vanity link per placement:** Save one brief file per send date (for example `2026-12-10-modaes-primary.md`) with one unique tracked link (`/for/<partner>` or `/es/para/<partner>`) and matching `ref` code for non-clickable podcast audio.
2. **Host-voice brief (2 to 3 bullets, zero stiff scripts):** Focus on one clear angle per slot (for example: no tech pack needed to start, physical colour swatch and sample in hand before cutting begins, direct founder supervision across Kolkata, Kanpur, Agra, Chennai, and Maharashtra, mutual NDA available).
3. **One call to action:** Route B2B audiences to a free 15-minute discovery call with Alma (`/book-a-call?duration=15`) or the 3-step Product Brief (`/product-brief`) via the vanity landing page. Never pitch discounts or prices.
4. **Mandatory disclosure:** Confirm written `"Sponsored by KarmaRise D Global"` / `"Patrocinado por KarmaRise D Global"` or spoken podcast disclosure is included.
5. **Make-good clause:** If a placement is omitted, ships with a broken link, or delivers fewer than 50% of the publisher's stated median clicks due to delivery or placement error, the publisher provides one replacement send or episode read at no extra fee within 30 days.
6. **Measurement window and dark-social capture:** Evaluate B2B placements on **Cost per Qualified Product Brief or Booked Call** across a **30 to 60 day cohort**, combining UTM attribution with the form `"How did you hear about us?"` (`referralSource`) field to capture listeners who search `"KarmaRise"` days later.

## 5. Partner UTM and vanity URL taxonomy

Keep partner tracking separate from owned email (`brevo` / `resend`) so reporting reconciles cleanly across Resend internal alerts, Cal.com booking metadata, and Brevo events:

| Parameter      | Format                                           | Examples                                                                  |
| :------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `utm_source`   | Lowercase partner or creator slug                | `b2b-retail-podcast`, `modaes-newsletter`, `maria-madrid`                 |
| `utm_medium`   | Fixed partner channel enum                       | `podcast`, `newsletter-sponsor`, `creator`, `gifting`, `linkedin-partner` |
| `utm_campaign` | `{track}-{wave}-{lang}`                          | `b2b-q1-es`, `b2b-podcast-intro`, `almarise-seeding-es`                   |
| `utm_content`  | Slot or creative format                          | `midroll-1`, `primary-slot`, `reel-bio`, `story-link`, `vanity-landing`   |
| `ref`          | Short memorable code for spoken audio or Stories | `retail-podcast`, `madrid-wearers`                                        |

Adding a new partner vanity page takes one typed entry inside `PARTNER_LANDINGS` in `src/data/partner-landings.ts`; Next.js statically pre-renders both `/for/<slug>` (EN) and `/es/para/<slug>` (ES) automatically.
