# Translation style guide

Status: approved

## Voice and tone

- Voice: Warm, friendly, professional, engaging, welcoming, and grounded human writing from founders Alma (ALMA.md) and Shirish (SHIRISH.md), matching CONTEXT.md and LANGUAGE.md. Frame KarmaRise around client-facing benefits across all four manufacturing categories including home decor (ofrecer moda a medida, Seguimos tu pedido, boxes arriving en la puerta de tu negocio) and genuine craft enthusiasm without hollow hype, exclamation-point spam, or AI filler words. Use operational facility terms (production sites, production units, manufacturing units, facilities / unidades de producción, puntos de producción, lugares de producción) alongside existing craft-specific nouns (Manufacturing Partners, family-run factories, leather ateliers, tanneries, garment makers, artisans, women artisan collectives, on the ground / fábricas de corte familiar, marroquineros, curtidurías, confeccionistas, sobre el terreno, a pie de obra), while avoiding workshop floor, sales floor, a pie de taller, or repetitive workshops/talleres. Avoid bulk cutting, cortar la producción, filtrar muestras, overly technical woven clothing / tejido plano in general marketing copy, and secretive en privado phrasing; clarify that KarmaRise guides dye requirements, creates a physical colour swatch for client approval, reviews every sample first, uses high-quality Japanese plastic YKK zippers (one single spec), and backs confidentiality with explicit mutual NDA availability. In es-ES, author natively from inside Spain with idiomatic retail and trade vocabulary: use whichever of piel or cuero feels most natural in context, prefer curtido vegetal over curtición, and use comercios, subir el cierre de tu tienda cada día, comprar a un mayorista, conclusiones de la reunión (rather than stiff acta in web copy), prendas de diversos tejidos / ropa de tejidos naturales, prendas de uso diario (never capas), dar el salto (in written web copy), corte incómodo (rather than rígido), bolso, bolsones, shrug/shrugs, vaqueros, móvil, ordenador, correo, with zero English UI calques (never prerrellenar, deja tu categoría preseleccionada, pistas de mostrador, vender directo al escaparate, or idea de ropa).
- Formality: Warm, direct Peninsular Spanish using consistent tú and plural vosotros (revisáis, vuestro equipo, podéis) across website pages, UI, FAQs, Stories, AlmaRise D Brand, and emails; never switch mid-sentence between 3rd-person subjects and 1st-person verbs (e.g. los dos fundadores trabajamos), never drift from tú into 3rd-person su colección, and never use Latin American ustedes in marketing copy. Allow formal usted strictly on statutory legal and policy pages (legal-notice, privacy-policy, terms, cookie-policy) where Spanish legal convention calls for it.
- Audience relationship: Peer-to-peer founder and independent retailer partnership built on warmth, transparency, straight dealing, NDA-backed confidentiality, and KarmaRise carrying operational follow-through (Seguimos tu pedido), never condescending charity, stiff corporate jargon, or hard-sell urgency.

## Conventions

- Capitalization: Sentence case for headings in both en-GB and es-ES. Follow native Spanish capitalization in es-ES (lowercase months, days, languages, and demonyms such as maratí, español, agosto). Preserve exact KarmaRise and AlmaRise D Brand capitalization (KarmaRise D Global, AlmaRise D Brand, AlmaRise D Reborn, AlmaRise D Organic, AlmaRise D Home Decor, Manufacturing Programme, Bulk Order; never use standalone AlmaRise or Almarise in visible copy while keeping lowercase /almarise URL routes).
- Punctuation: Never use em dashes in either language; use periods, commas, or colons instead. Always use opening and closing Spanish question and exclamation marks (¿...? and ¡...!) and natural Spanish quotation marks where fitting.
- Numbers, dates, and currency: In en-GB use day-month-year (15 May 2025), comma thousands separators (1,000), and leading currency symbols (€100). In es-ES use Spanish date format (15 de mayo de 2025), dot thousands separators (1.000), comma decimals, and trailing currency with space (100 €). Use metric units across both locales.
- Inclusive language: In es-ES use natural neutral rephrasing where effortless (quienes, tu equipo, personas) plus occasional natural pairs when speaking warmly (emprendedores y emprendedoras), or clientas specifically when narrating a women's retail shopper scene; avoid slash, @, or x forms.

## Surface constraints

- Length limits: Keep Spanish UI buttons, navigation links, card titles, and meta descriptions concise enough to match en-GB layout budgets (typically within +15% to +20% of en-GB length, SEO titles under 60 chars, meta descriptions under 160 chars). Mention Alma's spoken languages (Spanish, English, Portuguese) only where specifically relevant, such as Q&A/FAQs, call-booking pages, and contact contexts, never as boilerplate across Hero or general sections.
- Accessibility: Localize all image alt text, aria-label attributes, form labels, and validation messages accurately in the target language, describing visible scene details plainly without repeating workshop jargon.
- Formatting: Preserve all code tokens, URLs, root-relative routes (/es/..., /almarise), JSX interpolation, Markdown emphasis, italics on Indian vernacular terms, and email template variables without double-escaping. Always write Spain and the rest of Europe (España y el resto de Europa / España o en algún lugar de Europa) rather than framing Spain as outside Europe, and name Kolkata (Calcuta), Kanpur, Agra, and Chennai when detailing Indian craft hubs.
