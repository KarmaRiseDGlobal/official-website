# Translation project brief

Status: approved

## Product

- Name: KarmaRise D Global (including AlmaRise D Brand and the planned Women's Training Initiative)
- Summary: Bilingual Spain-India product development and managed manufacturing partner (Manufacturing Programmes) across India (including craft hubs in Kolkata, Kanpur, Agra, and Chennai, with supervision from Maharashtra) for Clients in Spain and the rest of Europe across four core categories (leather bags and other leather goods, clothing in natural and varied fabrics, custom sewn accessories, and home decor), plus owned fashion and lifestyle brand AlmaRise D Brand (AlmaRise D Reborn kept-saree everyday garments, AlmaRise D Organic plant/fruit-waste textiles among others, and AlmaRise D Home Decor) and the planned Women's Training Initiative.
- Audience: Independent retail shops (comercios), emerging and established fashion and home-decor brands, and retail chain buyers in Spain (primary market) and the rest of Europe; active women in Spain and the rest of Europe for AlmaRise D Brand; specialist family-run production units and women artisan collectives in India.

## Scope

- Source content: Next.js bilingual web UI and landing pages (src/app/, src/components/, src/data/home-content.ts, src/data/manufacturing-content.ts, src/data/how-we-work-content.ts, src/data/about-content.ts, src/data/conversion-content.ts, src/lib/seo.ts, src/lib/structured-data.ts, public/llms.txt), Sanity CMS and fallback editorial Stories (src/data/stories.ts), biweekly Brevo newsletters (src/data/newsletter-issues.ts), Resend transactional emails, and website assistant prompts (src/lib/chat.ts).
- Surfaces: Conversion and landing pages (/, /manufacturing, /how-we-work, /product-brief, /book-a-call), 4-category product and craft capability tables, founder and brand storytelling (/about, /almarise, /stories, biweekly newsletters), process/FAQ and NDA reassurance blocks, statutory legal and policy pages, contact flows (/contact), Resend/Brevo emails, AI chat responses, and llms.txt.
- Domains: Own-brand leather goods, natural/varied-fabric clothing, custom sewn accessory, and home decor manufacturing, retail product ergonomics (strap drop, linings, high-quality Japanese plastic YKK zippers, EU labelling), sustainable fashion and lifestyle (AlmaRise D Brand, AlmaRise D Reborn, AlmaRise D Organic, AlmaRise D Home Decor), Indian regional craft hubs (Kolkata, Kanpur, Agra, Chennai, Maharashtra), cross-border Spain-India operations, and statutory legal disclosures (India LLP Act, Spain LSSI-CE, GDPR, NDA/NNN).
- Constraints: Strict en-GB source spelling and native Peninsular Spanish (es-ES) transcreation with zero literal EN-to-ES calques; always spell the owned label AlmaRise D Brand (and AlmaRise D Reborn, AlmaRise D Organic, AlmaRise D Home Decor) in visible copy while keeping URL routes (/almarise) lowercase; write Spain and the rest of Europe (España y el resto de Europa) rather than implying Spain is outside Europe; use operational facility terms (production sites, production units, manufacturing units, facilities / unidades de producción, puntos de producción) alongside existing craft-specific nouns while avoiding workshop floor, sales floor, a pie de taller, bulk cutting, cortar la producción, filtrar muestras, overly technical tejido plano in general marketing copy, and repetitive workshops/talleres; mention Alma's spoken languages only in Q&A/FAQs and booking/contact contexts where relevant, never as Hero or general boilerplate; allow whichever of piel or cuero feels most natural in Spanish context; describe zippers accurately as one spec (high-quality Japanese plastic YKK zippers / cremalleras japonesas YKK de plástico de alta calidad); keep consistent grammatical person and tú address; pair confidentiality with explicit NDA mention (deferring interactive NDA upload to a later release); represent all of India and name Kolkata (Calcuta), Kanpur, Agra, and Chennai when citing craft hubs; never print Ulhasnagar outside statutory legal/postal addresses of Karmarise Global LLP; never name confidential clients (such as Snoby); never add social media links; never publish pricing, commissions, or fixed MOQs; preserve JSX/TS, Markdown, Sanity Portable Text, and email placeholders verbatim.
