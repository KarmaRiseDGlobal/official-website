## Platform

## Users

- **Primary (B2B Manufacturing Programmes):** Independent retail boutiques (`comercios`), emerging and established fashion and home-decor brands, multi-store retail chains, and entrepreneurs launching an own-brand line in Spain (primary market) and the rest of Europe (including Portugal and neighbouring EU markets). Decision-makers include independent shop owners, brand founders, creative directors, and retail purchasing or merchandising leads who want differentiated own-brand products without losing control of ergonomics, colour, quality, or export delivery.
- **Secondary (AlmaRise D Brand B2C):** Women in Spain and the rest of Europe seeking breathable, versatile, one-of-a-kind everyday garments and home decor pieces rooted in natural textiles and Indian craft.
- **Supply-side (India):** Established family-run Indian factories (`fábricas de corte familiar`), production units, leather ateliers, tanneries, garment makers, and women artisan collectives willing to work transparently with open doors.

## Product Purpose

KarmaRise D Global develops and delivers own-brand leather bags and small leather goods, clothing in natural and varied fabrics, custom sewn accessories and brand presentation, and home decor for Clients in Spain and the rest of Europe through hands-on managed manufacturing across India, from initial Product Brief to doorstep delivery.

Alongside its core B2B Manufacturing Programmes, KarmaRise D Global owns **AlmaRise D Brand** (spanning **AlmaRise D Reborn** one-of-a-kind everyday garments cut from kept Indian sarees, _thevnitlya sadya_, **AlmaRise D Organic** garments using organic dyes and plant/fruit-waste fabrics such as sugarcane, orange, banana, and pineapple, among others, and **AlmaRise D Home Decor**) and is preparing the **Women's Training Initiative** in India (not yet open for applications; Section 8 foundation in preparation).

Success means winning qualified B2B enquiries across all four categories (through the 3-step, 1-minute **Product Brief** or a **15-minute / 30-minute Google Meet video call with Alma**), building an engaged bilingual audience for KarmaRise Stories and AlmaRise D Brand, and establishing commercial groundwork for the Women's Training Initiative.

## Positioning

KarmaRise D Global operates as one continuous bilingual founder team across both countries rather than a remote brokerage or B2B sourcing directory. Co-founders **Alma** (Spanish business owner working between Spain and India, handling European Client alignment and retail ergonomics) and **Shirish** (Indian lawyer and registered Bar Council advocate, structuring enforceable Indian supplier contracts, auditing Manufacturing Partners in person, and overseeing production) work together on the ground in India.

Unlike single-factory agents or mass _prêt-à-porter_ channels, KarmaRise matches every Product Brief across India's specialist craft hubs (**Kolkata**, **Kanpur**, **Agra**, and **Chennai**, with coordination and oversight from **Maharashtra**), guides dye requirements and creates physical colour swatches for Client approval, reviews and pushes back on physical samples before cutting starts, offers mutual NDA and NNN confidentiality, verifies EU labelling, and manages door-to-door export delivery. Operating AlmaRise D Brand gives both founders direct cutting, fabric, and finishing practice rather than detached intermediary oversight.

## Operating Context

- **Client intake and alignment:** Clients can start without finished tech packs or technical drawings using a 1-minute Product Brief (`/product-brief`), a rough sketch, reference photos, an existing shop item, or a 15-minute or 30-minute Google Meet call (`/book-a-call`), followed by written meeting conclusions (`conclusiones de la reunión`) from Alma.
- **Physical verification rituals:** Colour is approved via physical colour swatches rather than phone photos; Clients hold and approve a physical sample in hand before production cutting starts; live video updates connect Clients directly with makers on site; written sign-offs close each stage before the next begins.
- **Retail ergonomics and compliance:** Proactive advice covers strap drop (`caída del asa`), light cotton linings, antique brass hardware, high-quality Japanese plastic YKK zippers, sizing and drape, unbleached care hangtags, dust bags, REACH and EU sewn-in labelling, and export carton packing.
- **Active platform integrations:** Bilingual Next.js 16 web application deployed to Cloudflare Workers via OpenNext (`karmarisedglobal.com`), backed by Sanity CMS (Stories), Brevo (biweekly bilingual newsletter campaigns and track segmentation), Resend (transactional enquiry and booking confirmations), Cal booking slots, and Cloudflare Turnstile form protection.

## Product Principles

1. **Physical proof before production:** Every colour, material, and construction detail is verified through physical swatches and an approved physical sample in hand before cutting begins.
2. **Real founders, direct accountability:** Every Client interacts directly with Alma and Shirish, backed by enforceable Indian contracts, written meeting conclusions, and in-person site checks.
3. **Low-friction entry, thorough execution:** Clients never need finished tech packs to begin, while every downstream manufacturing, labelling, and export step is specified in writing.
4. **Native bilingual equality:** English (`en-GB`) and Peninsular Spanish (`es-ES`) are first-class native experiences with equal route coverage, natural retail idioms, and zero mechanical calques.
5. **Dignity and open doors across India:** Production happens only with audited family-run factories, specialist craft hubs, and women artisan collectives that welcome direct supervision and fair practice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Standard:** WCAG 2.2 AA across both English and Spanish surfaces, including 4.5:1 minimum contrast for normal text, `44px` minimum touch targets on all interactive controls, visible keyboard focus indicators, proper dialog focus trapping (`SiteMenu`), skip-to-content navigation, and full `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` fallbacks for scroll-pinned showcases, videos, and page transitions.
- **Bilingual text resilience:** Layouts must accommodate 15% to 25% Spanish text expansion without clipping, overlap, or horizontal overflow down to `320px` viewport width.
- **Direct human access:** Alternative low-friction paths (email fallback, WhatsApp link, 15-minute call booking) remain available alongside web forms so first-time overseas buyers and assistive-technology users are never blocked.
