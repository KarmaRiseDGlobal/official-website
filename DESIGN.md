# Design System: KarmaRise D Global

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Sunlit Atelier"**

KarmaRise D Global connects independent retail boutiques, fashion brands, and retail chains in Spain and the rest of Europe with specialist production units across India. The visual system brings that direct, hands-on partnership onto the screen through warm natural craft: unbleached parchment canvases, botanical forest grounds, raw timber surfaces, terracotta clay accents, and antique brass calls to action. Rather than staging cold industrial capacity or distant corporate prestige, every screen feels like sitting across a sunlit table with Alma and Shirish, examining vegetable-tanned hides, physical colour swatches, and sewn samples in natural light.

Pages breathe at an editorial pace. Wide outer gutters, unhurried vertical section gaps, and calm 12-column grids let real photography of founders, artisans, leather, and natural fabrics lead the eye before structured forms or specifications ask for a decision. Surface tone shifts carry narrative transitions across long pages, moving organically between Warm Parchment, Warm Dune, Pale Sage, Deep Forest, Raw Wood, and Deep Night while keeping both English (`en-GB`) and Peninsular Spanish (`es-ES`) copy legible and balanced.

The visual system explicitly rejects sterile B2B agency gloss, cold factory stock grids, neon startup gradients, and loud luxury excess. No social media links appear in site chrome, and every visual token supports warm, grounded, professional clarity without decoration for its own sake.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Warm editorial craft anchored in real natural materials: parchment, dune, botanical forest, terracotta clay, raw timber, and antique brass.
- Tonal layering first, using full-bleed surface shifts and hairline parchment rules for structure while reserving soft ambient shadows for elevated interactive controls and dialogs.
- One tactile shape family derived directly from the KarmaRise D Global logo: full-pill CTAs and chips, generously rounded 20px cards, softened 12px inputs, and arched editorial photo frames.
- Two-font editorial hierarchy pairing flared-serif Marcellus display headings (weight 400 only) with humanist-geometric Plus Jakarta Sans UI and body copy.
- Bilingual layout resilience engineered for English (`en-GB`) and Spanish (`es-ES`) expansion, maintaining 44px minimum touch targets and WCAG AA contrast across every surface.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the physical materials of an Indian-Spanish atelier: unbleached paper, vegetable-tanned hide, antique brass hardware, botanical leaf dye, raw timber, and deep night ground. Canonical values live in `src/app/tokens.css`, with CSS aliases (`--color-parchment` for `paper`, `--color-dune` for `paper-2`, `--color-sand` for `paper-3`, `--color-ink-2` for `wood`, and `--color-accent` for `clay`) preserved for semantic clarity.

### Primary

- **Antique Brass** (`#b99a58`, `--color-gold`): Primary conversion action color on dark surfaces (`.hero-primary`, `.primary-button`, `.header-desktop-cta` over dark heroes, active pills on wood/night forms, active progress fills, and eyebrows on dark sections). Always paired with Deep Night (`#111914`) text when used as a button fill.
- **Deep Forest** (`#182d20`, `--color-forest-deep`): Primary brand anchor, echoing the botanical logo. Serves as the primary display heading ink on light surfaces, primary CTA button fill on light surfaces, active calendar/chip fill on light surfaces, and the deep botanical dark band across showcase sections.
- **Botanical Forest** (`#35563a`, `--color-forest`): Hover and focus accent paired with Deep Forest on interactive light controls, active focus borders in the booking scheduler, and confirmation status indicators.
- **Lush Forest** (`#29482f`, `--color-forest-lush`): Mid-dark botanical tone reserved for layered forest depth and secondary dark gradients.

### Secondary

- **Deep Terracotta Ink** (`#833e28`, `--color-clay-ink`): Screen-adapted accessible terracotta text color on light surfaces (section indices, eyebrows, inline link hovers, form validation errors on light backgrounds, and hover background for Deep Forest primary buttons).

- **Warm Dune** (`#e6dac4`, `--color-paper-2` / `--color-dune`): Secondary warm light surface for alternating bands, editorial sidebars, media placeholder fills, inactive chips, and muted secondary copy on dark surfaces.

### Named rules

**The Adapted Clay Rule.** Raw Terracotta Clay (`#bd7057`) fails WCAG AA contrast for small text (3.2:1 on Warm Parchment, 2.7:1 on Pale Sage, 3.0:1 on Raw Wood). Restrict `#bd7057` strictly to underlines, dots, borders, and graphics. Use Deep Terracotta Ink (`#833e28`) for accent text on light surfaces and Pale Terracotta (`#eab099`) or Antique Brass (`#b99a58`) on dark surfaces.

**The Surface-Aware Primary Rule.** Primary conversion actions adapt to their surface contrast rather than forcing one flat fill everywhere: Antique Brass (`#b99a58`) with Deep Night (`#111914`) ink on dark grounds, and Deep Forest (`#182d20`) with Warm Parchment (`#f2ecdf`) ink on light grounds.

**The Organic Seam Rule.** High-contrast `SectionBridge` transitions between light (`paper`, `dune`, `pale-sage`) and dark (`forest-deep`, `wood`, `night`) grounds always use 9-stop `oklab` S-curve easing gradients (`0%, 14%, 28%, 42%, 56%, 70%, 84%, 94%, 100%` via `color-mix(in oklab, ...)` over canonical surface tokens) and `size="gentle"` (`clamp(128px, 22svh, 240px)`) on landing page seams, never short 4 to 5 stop linear `srgb` bands or undocumented hex stops.
Why: short linear `srgb` gradients create visible Mach-band creases and abrupt contrast cliffs between light and dark sections.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Marcellus (with `Georgia, serif` fallback, loaded via `--font-marcellus` at weight `400` only)
**Body Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (with `system-ui, sans-serif` fallback, loaded via `--font-jakarta` across regular through bold weights)
**Technical / Numeric Style:** Plus Jakarta Sans with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (`.num`, `.figure`, `td.num`, counters, calendar dates, LLPIN/GSTIN identifiers)

**Character:** Marcellus brings quiet classical authority with softly flared terminals that match craft heritage without sharp modernist coldness. Because Marcellus ships in a single regular weight (`400`) and loses legibility at small UI sizes, the system intentionally extends brand typography with Plus Jakarta Sans for all body prose, forms, buttons, navigation, and metadata. Its open humanist-geometric counters pair cleanly with Marcellus's generous proportions and with the site's rounded shape language. Two typefaces are the hard ceiling for KarmaRise D Global; do not introduce a third font family.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (`Marcellus`, weight `400`, `clamp(2.5rem, 4.2vw + 0.75rem, 4.5rem)`, line-height `1.06`, tracking `-0.012em`): Primary `h1` hero headlines and marquee section statements. Constrained to `20ch` max width with `text-wrap: balance`.
- **Display Small** (`Marcellus`, weight `400`, `clamp(2rem, 3.3vw + 0.5rem, 3.25rem)`, line-height `1.04`, tracking `-0.012em`): Secondary page titles, founder section headings, and Process header titles.
- **Section / Headline** (`Marcellus`, weight `400`, `clamp(1.875rem, 2.3vw + 0.9rem, 3.125rem)`, line-height `1.06`, tracking `-0.012em`): Standard `h2` section titles across landing, capability, and FAQ bands, capped at `30ch`.
- **Title** (`Marcellus`, weight `400`, `clamp(1.25rem, 0.65vw + 0.9rem, 1.5625rem)`, line-height `1.18`, tracking `-0.01em`): Card headings (`h3`), FAQ questions, and Story subheadings, capped at `40ch`. Fixed modular scale helpers `--text-lg` (`1.25rem`), `--text-xl` (`1.5625rem`), `--text-2xl` (`1.953rem`), and `--text-3xl` (`2.441rem`) follow a 1.25 major-third ratio.
- **Lead** (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, weight `400`, `clamp(1.125rem, 0.35vw + 1rem, 1.3125rem)`, line-height `1.45`): Editorial introductory paragraphs (`.lead`), constrained to `52ch` max width.
- **Body** (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, weight `400`, `1rem` / `16px`, line-height `1.62`): Standard reading copy (`--text-md`), constrained to `62ch`-`65ch` (`--measure-copy`) with `text-wrap: pretty`, and `42ch` (`--measure-narrow`) in split editorial columns.
- **Body Small** (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, weight `400`-`500`, `0.875rem` / `14px`, line-height `1.62`): Navigation links, card summaries, form labels, secondary links, and footer contact lists (`--text-sm`).
- **Label / Eyebrow** (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, weight `600`, `0.8125rem` / `13px`, line-height `1.4`, `--tracking-label: 0.06em` for metadata/chips/buttons and `--tracking-eyebrow: 0.1em` for uppercase section eyebrows): Section indices, category badges, chips, form hints, and legal footnotes (`--text-xs`). Never drop below `0.8125rem` (`13px`) on readable UI labels.

### Named rules

**The Honest Regular Rule.** Marcellus loads strictly at weight `400` with `font-synthesis: none` declared on `h1, h2, h3`. Never apply `font-medium` (`500`) or `font-semibold` (`600`) to Marcellus elements; scale hierarchy through size (`--text-display`, `--text-section`, `--text-title`) and contrast instead of synthetic weight.

**The Two-Font Ceiling Rule.** All screens use Marcellus for display/editorial headings and Plus Jakarta Sans for UI, controls, and reading copy. Monospace is never added; tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) handle dates, counters, and legal registration numbers cleanly inside Plus Jakarta Sans.

**The Scale Discipline Rule.** Choose typography sizes from the `tokens.css` modular scale (`--text-xs` through `--text-3xl` and fluid `--text-display`, `--text-display-s`, `--text-section`, `--text-title`, `--text-lead`). Live localhost capture (`output/localhost/DESIGN.md`) revealed over 35 computed text-size variants caused by one-off `clamp()` overrides and `0.68rem` micro-labels; consolidate off-scale values back onto the canonical scale.

## Layout

The site runs on a centered fluid container (`--container: 1440px`) with responsive horizontal gutters (`--gutter`), a 12-column CSS Grid system (`lg:grid-cols-12` or explicit fractional splits such as `5fr / 7fr`, `7fr / 5fr`, and `4fr / 4fr / 4fr`), and `min-width: 0` grid children to prevent overflow under longer Spanish (`es-ES`) copy.

### Layering stack (`z-index`)

Canonical stacking tokens prevent chrome collisions across fixed headers, overlays, chat drawers, and tooltips:

- `--z-base: 1`, `--z-raised: 10`, `--z-dropdown: 100`, `--z-sticky: 200`, `--z-sticky-nav: 300`, `--z-modal: 400`, `--z-toast: 500`, `--z-tooltip: 600`, `--z-loader: 1000` (with `.page-transition` at `calc(var(--z-loader) - 10)`).

### Bilingual expansion headroom

Peninsular Spanish copy routinely runs 15% to 25% longer than English. Navigation bars (`>= 72rem`), pill CTAs, two-column form rows, and footer link groups must wrap gracefully or use fluid gaps (`clamp(1rem, 1.8vw, 1.85rem)`) without clipping or forcing horizontal scroll down to `320px`.

## Elevation & Depth

KarmaRise D Global is **tonally layered first and softly lifted second**. Page architecture relies primarily on full-bleed tonal plane changes (Warm Parchment, Warm Dune, Pale Sage, Deep Forest, Raw Wood, Deep Night) separated by 1px Parchment Rules (`--color-rule`) or smooth `SectionBridge` gradient seams. Surfaces remain flat at rest so photography and typography carry the page without floating card clutter.

Diffuse, warm-tinted ambient shadows (`color-mix()` over Raw Wood or Deep Night, never cold pure black) appear selectively on elevated interactive tool and chrome objects: the booking scheduler, the scrolled floating header pill, the floating chat widget and drawer, showcase panels, and reference popovers. Floating glass chrome (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` to `blur(18px)`) is restricted to overlays sitting over photography or scrolling content (scrolled navigation pill, dark enquiry glass cards, and desktop stage readout bars).

### Shadow vocabulary

- **Card Ambient Lift (`--shadow-card`)** (`box-shadow: 0 2rem 5rem color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-wood) 12%, transparent)`): Canonical diffuse shadow for elevated light cards, scheduler surfaces, floating chat launcher, and chat drawer.
- **Popover and Floating Chrome Lift (`--shadow-popover`)** (`box-shadow: 0 1rem 2.5rem color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-night) 24%, transparent)`): Crisp short-range elevation for the scrolled floating header pill (`90%` Deep Night glass with `blur(18px)`), language switcher menu, and inline Story reference popovers and mobile sheets.
- **Dark Showcase Panel Lift (`--shadow-panel`)** (`box-shadow: 0 1.75rem 4.5rem color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-night) 32%, transparent)`): Deep ambient separation for active horizontal showcase slices and the dark enquiry panel card.

### Named rules

**The Warm Shadow Rule.** Never cast pure `#000000` or neutral cool-grey shadows. Tint every shadow from `--color-wood`, `--color-forest-deep`, or `--color-night` using `color-mix()` so elevation blends with parchment and timber surfaces.

**The Tonal Plane First Rule.** Reach for a surface shift (`paper` to `paper-2` or `pale-sage`) and a `1px` `--color-rule` border before adding a drop shadow. Reserve `--shadow-card` for interactive surfaces, floating chrome, and key conversion cards.

## Shapes

Every corner, contour, and icon in the system traces directly to the KarmaRise D Global logo (`public/brand/main-logo-transparent.png`): three concentric rounded-cap semicircular arches above a curved stem ending in vertical pill droplets, botanical leaves sprouting off a horizontal bar, and softly rounded letterforms. That silhouette defines one unified, tactile, welcoming shape language across the entire website.

### Radius ladder by surface scale

Perceived roundness scales with container area so small controls and large surfaces feel part of the same family:

- **Full Pill (`--radius-pill: 999px`):** All primary and secondary buttons, selection/filter chips, Story tags and share buttons, status badges, floating navigation shell, language switcher trigger, and circular icon buttons. Directly echoes the logo's rounded-cap strokes and pill droplets.
- **Softened Control (`--radius-input: 0.75rem` / `12px`):** Form text inputs, textareas, `<select>` dropdowns, calendar day/slot buttons, option cards, file dropzones, glossary cards, editorial Story image frames, and nested sub-panels inside cards.

- **Nested Corner Rule:** When an element sits inside a padded rounded container (such as media inside a showcase card or an inner scroll box inside a 1px bordered popover), reduce the inner radius by the padding or border thickness (`outerRadius - inset`) so curves stay concentric.

### Iconography

### Named rules

**The Single Shape Family Rule.** Buttons, chips, and floating bars are full pills (`--radius-pill: 999px`); inputs, glossary cards, and nested controls are `12px` (`--radius-input: 0.75rem`); popovers and chat bubbles are `16px` (`--radius-panel: 1rem`); cards and drawers are `20px` (`--radius-card: 1.25rem`); showcase portraits use `--radius-arch`. Never introduce sharp `0px` interactive controls or arbitrary intermediate radii outside this ladder.

**The Concentric Nesting Rule.** Never place a `20px` card inside a `20px` card or a sharp square inside a `20px` rounded shell. Step inner radii down (`card 20px` -> `nested media or sub-card 12px`) so corners track cleanly.

## Components

Components feel **tactile, welcoming, and direct**: generous 44px-54px hit areas, clear hover inversions, visible focus rings, and crisp motion tuned to `--dur-micro` (`120ms`), `--dur-short` (`220ms`), `--dur-long` (`420ms`), and `--dur-slow` (`650ms`) with `--ease-out` (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). All animations collapse cleanly under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use CSS custom properties from `src/app/tokens.css` (`var(--color-*)`, `var(--text-*)`, `var(--space-*)`, `var(--radius-*)`, `var(--shadow-card)`, `var(--tap-min)`, `var(--control-height)`, `var(--z-*)`, `var(--dur-*)`, `var(--ease-*)`) across every component and stylesheet.
- **Do** pair Antique Brass (`#b99a58`) primary buttons with Deep Night (`#111914`) text on dark grounds, and Deep Forest (`#182d20`) primary buttons with Warm Parchment (`#f2ecdf`) text on light grounds.
- **Do** use Deep Terracotta Ink (`#833e28`) for small accent text on light surfaces and Pale Terracotta (`#eab099`) or Antique Brass (`#b99a58`) on dark surfaces (The Adapted Clay Rule).
- **Do** enforce `44px` (`--tap-min`) minimum height on every interactive button, chip, link, and calendar cell, and `54px` (`--control-height`) on primary CTAs and form fields.
- **Do** keep Marcellus strictly at weight `400` and apply `tabular-nums` (`.num`) to every counter, step number, date, and registration code.
- **Do** unify all UI outline SVGs on `stroke="currentColor"`, `strokeWidth="1.6"`, `strokeLinecap="round"`, and `strokeLinejoin="round"`, and apply pill (`999px`) or rounded (`12px`/`20px`) corners consistently across all routes including Stories.
- **Do** test every layout and button label in both English (`en-GB`) and Spanish (`es-ES`) down to `320px` width to catch wrapping and overflow before shipping.

### Don't:

- **Don't** hardcode hex colors, `rgba()` strings, arbitrary `shadow-[...]` values, or uncalibrated intermediate gradient hex stops inside `src/components/`; compose blends with `color-mix()` over canonical surface tokens.
- **Don't** use raw Terracotta Clay (`#bd7057`) for small text, labels, or error messages where contrast falls below WCAG AA 4.5:1.
- **Don't** apply `font-medium` (`500`) or `font-semibold` (`600`) to Marcellus headings, and don't drop readable UI labels below `0.8125rem` (`--text-xs`).
- **Don't** mix sharp `0px` buttons, cards, or chips into the rounded pill-and-card shape language, or invent arbitrary intermediate radii (`0.6rem`, `0.85rem`, `0.9rem`) outside the `--radius-input` / `--radius-panel` / `--radius-card` / `--radius-pill` ladder.
- **Don't** add a third font family, pure `#000000` drop shadows, social media profile links in site chrome, or generic B2B SaaS gradients and icons.
- **Don't** ship interactive pill controls at `34px`, `36px`, `38px`, `40px`, or `42px` height when `--tap-min` (`44px`) is the accessibility floor.
