## 2. Structure of a Manufacturing Programme

KarmaRise manages the end-to-end development, sampling, production supervision, quality inspection, and dispatch readiness of the Client's own-brand products in India across four sequential phases:

- **Phase 01 — Design Approval:** Alignment on the written **Product Brief**, bill of materials, dimensions, hardware/trims, target cost, and packaging specifications.
- **Phase 02 — Sample Approval:** Development and physical inspection of prototypes until the Client approves and signs off in writing on the reference pre-production sample (the **"Golden Sample"**).
- **Phase 03 — Production:** Placement of the **Bulk Order** with a vetted Indian **Manufacturing Partner** matched to the product's material and volume, with on-the-ground floor supervision, dye-batch verification, and construction monitoring by KarmaRise in India.
- **Phase 04 — Shipping & Pre-Shipment Inspection:** Final inspection of finished goods, labelling, barcode/care tags, and export packing against the Golden Sample before handover under the agreed Incoterms.

Each individual Bulk Order under this Agreement shall be documented in a written **Purchase Order / Programme Schedule** signed by both Parties.

---

## 3. Golden Sample Standard & Manufacturing Tolerances

1. **No Bulk Cut Without Sample Approval:** KarmaRise shall not authorize bulk material cutting or Phase 03 production until the Client has approved the Golden Sample in writing.
2. **Duplicate Golden Samples:** Two identical Golden Samples shall be sealed: one held by the Client and one held on the production floor in India by KarmaRise for side-by-side inspection during Phase 03 and Phase 04.
3. **Handcraft & Textile Tolerances:** Unless otherwise specified in the Product Brief, industry-standard tolerances apply (`±1.0 cm` on garment/bag dimensions and natural grain or vegetable-dye shade variation within approved shade bands).

## 4. Pricing, Payment Milestones & Invoicing

Unless otherwise stated in a specific Programme Schedule, all Bulk Orders are invoiced by **Karmarise Global LLP** (`accounts@karmarisedglobal.com`) in **`[EUR / USD]`** under the following milestone schedule:

1. **Deposit (50% of Bulk Order Value):** Due upon written Golden Sample approval and issuance of the Bulk Order invoice to initiate raw material procurement and line booking. Production lead time begins on the business day the deposit clears.
2. **Balance Payment (50% of Bulk Order Value + Agreed Freight/Testing):** Due within **five (5) business days** of KarmaRise delivering the passed **Pre-Shipment Quality Inspection Report** (including high-resolution photographs and video of packed goods) and prior to physical release/dispatch of the shipment.
3. **Taxes & Duties:** Export prices from India are quoted net of destination country import duties, Spanish/EU VAT (_IVA_), and customs brokerage unless **DDP** Incoterms are explicitly agreed in the Programme Schedule.

## 5. Quality Inspection, AQL Standards & Remedies

1. **In-Line & Pre-Shipment Inspection:** Shirish and KarmaRise's India team conduct in-line floor checks during Phase 03 and a final pre-shipment inspection during Phase 04 based on **ISO 2859-1 (AQL 2.5 Major / 4.0 Minor)** unless a stricter standard is specified in the Product Brief.
2. **Pre-Dispatch Rectification:** Any units failing pre-shipment inspection in India are repaired or remade at the workshop prior to packing at no additional cost to the Client.
3. **Post-Arrival Claims:** The Client shall inspect delivered goods within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of arrival at destination and notify KarmaRise (`support@karmarisedglobal.com` and `hola@karmarisedglobal.com`) with photographic evidence of any manufacturing defect exceeding agreed AQL tolerances. For verified manufacturing defects attributable to production, KarmaRise shall, at the Client's election, provide a replacement run, rework credit, or pro-rata credit note on the defective units.

## 6. Delivery, Lead Times & Incoterms (ICC Incoterms® 2020)

1. **Incoterms:** Each Programme Schedule shall specify the delivery term (**EXW**, **FOB Mumbai / Nhava Sheva**, **CIF**, or **DDP `[Destination City, Spain/EU]`**). Risk and title transfer in accordance with ICC Incoterms® 2020, provided that title to the goods shall not pass to the Client until full payment of the Bulk Order invoice is received by Karmarise Global LLP.
2. **Testing & Labelling:** Destination market compliance requirements (such as EU textile fibre composition regulations, REACH material testing, or custom care labels in Spanish/English/Portuguese) must be specified in the Product Brief prior to Phase 03.

## 7. Intellectual Property, Exclusivity & Non-Circumvention

1. **Client IP Ownership:** All Client designs, patterns, tech packs, brand labels, and custom tooling paid for by the Client remain the exclusive property of the Client and shall never be produced for or shown to third parties.
2. **Protection of Manufacturing Partner Network:** During the term of this Agreement and for **thirty-six (36) months** after the final Bulk Order delivery, the Client shall not circumvent KarmaRise by contacting or placing orders directly or indirectly with any Indian Manufacturing Partner or supplier introduced by KarmaRise, subject to the liquidated damages (25% of gross order value) set out in the Mutual NDA/NNN Agreement.

---

## 8. Limitation of Liability & Force Majeure

1. **Liability Cap:** Each Party's aggregate liability under any Bulk Order (other than for payment of invoices, breach of confidentiality, IP infringement, or non-circumvention) shall not exceed **100% of the total invoice value** of the specific Bulk Order giving rise to the claim.
2. **Force Majeure:** Neither Party shall be liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control (including natural disasters, port closures, sovereign export/import restrictions, or severe utility disruptions), provided the affected Party notifies the other within 5 business days and takes all reasonable steps to mitigate delay.
