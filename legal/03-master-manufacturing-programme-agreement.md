## 2. Six-Stage Manufacturing Programme Structure

KarmaRise manages the end-to-end development, sampling, production supervision, quality inspection, and dispatch readiness of the Client's own-brand products in India across six sequential stages:

- **Stage 01: Brief and Product Study.** Alignment on the written **Product Brief**, bill of materials, retail ergonomics, hardware and trim specifications, target cost, and packaging expectations.
- **Stage 02: Material and Physical Swatch Approval.** Partner matching across India's specialist craft hubs (including Kolkata, Kanpur, Agra, and Chennai, overseen from Maharashtra) and preparation of physical leather or fabric colour swatches for written Client approval.
- **Stage 03: Physical Sample Review and Sign-Off.** Development, in-person review in India, and physical inspection of prototypes until the Client approves and signs off in writing on the reference pre-production sample (the **"Golden Sample"**).
- **Stage 04: Contracts and Pre-Production Prep.** Execution of written Indian supplier contracts with the vetted **Manufacturing Partner**, locking raw materials, dye lots, antique brass hardware, high-quality Japanese plastic YKK zippers, woven labels, and EU compliance specs prior to cutting.
- **Stage 05: Supervised Production and On-Site Checks.** Placement and execution of the **Bulk Order** at the matched Indian production unit, with on-site production supervision, dye-batch verification, in-line quality checks, and live progress updates by KarmaRise in India.
- **Stage 06: EU Labelling, Packing, and Door-to-Door Delivery.** Final inspection of finished goods, sewn-in EU fibre or leather composition labels, care hangtags, dust bags, export carton packing, and customs clearance handover under the agreed Incoterms.

Each individual Bulk Order under this Agreement shall be documented in a written **Purchase Order / Programme Schedule** signed by both Parties.

## 3. Golden Sample Standard & Manufacturing Tolerances

1. **No Production Without Sample Approval:** KarmaRise shall not authorize production cutting or Stage 05 manufacturing until the Client has approved the physical Golden Sample in writing.
2. **Duplicate Golden Samples:** Two identical Golden Samples shall be sealed: one held by the Client and one held at the Indian production site by KarmaRise for side-by-side inspection during Stage 05 and Stage 06.
3. **Handcraft & Textile Tolerances:** Unless otherwise specified in the Product Brief, industry-standard tolerances apply (±1.0 cm on garment, accessory, home decor, or bag dimensions and natural grain or vegetable-dye shade variation within approved shade bands).

## 4. Pricing, Payment Milestones, Taxes & Invoicing

Unless otherwise stated in a specific Programme Schedule, all Bulk Orders are invoiced by **Karmarise Global LLP** (accounts@karmarisedglobal.com) in **[EUR / USD]** under the following milestone schedule:

1. **Deposit (50% of Bulk Order Value):** Due upon written Golden Sample approval (Stage 03) and issuance of the Stage 04 Bulk Order invoice to initiate raw material procurement and production scheduling. Production lead time begins on the business day the deposit clears.
2. **Balance Payment (50% of Bulk Order Value + Agreed Freight / Testing):** Due within **five (5) business days** of KarmaRise delivering the passed **Pre-Shipment Quality Inspection Report** (including high-resolution photographs and video of packed goods) and prior to physical release or dispatch of the shipment.
3. **Taxes & Duties (Including Spanish and EU VAT / IVA):** Export prices from India are quoted net of destination country import duties, Spanish and European Union VAT (_IVA_), and customs brokerage unless **DDP** Incoterms are explicitly agreed in the Programme Schedule.

## 5. Quality Inspection, AQL Standards & Remedies

1. **In-Line & Pre-Shipment Inspection:** Shirish and KarmaRise's India team conduct in-line on-site checks during Stage 05 and a final pre-shipment inspection during Stage 06 against the Golden Sample based on **ISO 2859-1 (AQL 2.5 Major / 4.0 Minor)** unless a stricter standard is specified in the Product Brief.
2. **Pre-Dispatch Rectification:** Any units failing pre-shipment inspection in India are repaired or remade at the production unit prior to packing at no additional cost to the Client.
3. **Post-Arrival Claims:** The Client shall inspect delivered goods within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of arrival at destination and notify KarmaRise (support@karmarisedglobal.com and hola@karmarisedglobal.com) with photographic evidence of any manufacturing defect exceeding agreed AQL tolerances. For verified manufacturing defects attributable to production, KarmaRise shall, at the Client's election, provide a replacement run, rework credit, or pro-rata credit note on the defective units.

## 7. Intellectual Property, Exclusivity & Non-Circumvention

1. **Client IP Ownership:** All Client designs, patterns, tech packs, brand labels, and custom tooling paid for by the Client remain the exclusive intellectual property of the Client and shall never be produced for or shown to third parties or used for KarmaRise's own brands (including **AlmaRise D Brand**, **AlmaRise D Reborn**, **AlmaRise D Organic**, and **AlmaRise D Home Decor**).
2. **Protection of Manufacturing Partner Network:** During the term of this Agreement and for **thirty-six (36) months** after the final Bulk Order delivery, the Client shall not circumvent KarmaRise by contacting or placing orders directly or indirectly with any Indian Manufacturing Partner, production unit, or supplier introduced by KarmaRise, subject to the liquidated damages (25% of gross order value or KarmaRise's standard programme margin, whichever is greater) set out in the Mutual NDA / NNN Agreement.

## 8. Limitation of Liability & Force Majeure

1. **Liability Cap:** Each Party's aggregate liability under any Bulk Order (other than for payment of invoices, breach of confidentiality, IP infringement, or non-circumvention) shall not exceed **100% of the total invoice value** of the specific Bulk Order giving rise to the claim.
2. **Force Majeure:** Neither Party shall be liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control (including natural disasters, port closures, sovereign export or import restrictions, or severe utility disruptions), provided the affected Party notifies the other in writing within **five (5) business days** and takes all reasonable steps to mitigate delay.
