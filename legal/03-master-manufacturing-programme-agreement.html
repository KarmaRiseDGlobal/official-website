1. Parties

This Master Manufacturing Programme Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between Karmarise Global LLP (LLPIN: ACO-3878, GSTIN: 27ABDFK8128R1ZL), registered at 2nd floor, Guru Charan Apartment, Ulhasnagar, Maharashtra, India, 421002, operating the KarmaRise D Global brand ("KarmaRise"), and [Client Legal Entity Name], registered in [Spain / Country] under tax/VAT ID [NIF / CIF / VAT Number], with registered address at [Client Registered Address] (the "Client").

2. Four-Phase Manufacturing Programme Structure

KarmaRise develops, coordinates, supervises, and delivers the Client's commissioned products through vetted Indian Manufacturing Partners across four phases: Phase 01 (Design Approval), Phase 02 (Sample Approval), Phase 03 (Bulk Order Production), and Phase 04 (Pre-Shipment Inspection & Shipping). Each Bulk Order is confirmed via a signed Purchase Order / Programme Schedule referencing this Agreement.

3. Golden Sample Standard & Production Tolerances

3.1 No Bulk Cut Without Approval: Phase 03 bulk material cutting shall not begin until the Client signs off in writing on the physical Counter Sample (the "Golden Sample"). Duplicate sealed Golden Samples are held by the Client and by KarmaRise's production supervision team in India.
3.2 Tolerances: Unless otherwise stated in the Product Brief, standard textile and leather craft tolerances of ±1.0 cm on dimensions and approved dye-lot shade bands apply.

4. Pricing, Payment Milestones & Invoicing

All invoices are issued by Karmarise Global LLP (accounts@karmarisedglobal.com) in EUR (€):

  • 50% Deposit: Payable upon Golden Sample approval and issuance of the Bulk Order invoice to commence material procurement and production scheduling.
  • 50% Balance + Agreed Freight/Testing: Payable within 5 business days of KarmaRise delivering the passed Pre-Shipment Quality Inspection Report (with photographs and video of packed goods) prior to shipment release.

5. Quality Inspection (AQL), Pre-Shipment Checks & Remedies

Shirish and KarmaRise's team in India conduct in-line floor checks during Phase 03 and a final pre-shipment inspection during Phase 04 against the Golden Sample using ISO 2859-1 (AQL 2.5 Major / 4.0 Minor). Units failing pre-shipment inspection in India are repaired or remade before dispatch at no cost to the Client. Post-arrival claims for latent manufacturing defects must be submitted with photographic evidence within 14 calendar days of delivery to support@karmarisedglobal.com; verified defects are remedied via replacement run, rework, or pro-rata credit note.

6. Delivery Incoterms (ICC Incoterms® 2020)

Each Programme Schedule specifies the agreed Incoterm (FOB Mumbai / EXW / DDP Spain). Title to the goods transfers to the Client upon receipt of full payment of the Bulk Order invoice by Karmarise Global LLP.

7. Client IP Ownership & 36-Month Non-Circumvention

All Client designs, patterns, tech packs, trademarks, and paid custom tooling remain the exclusive intellectual property of the Client and shall never be produced for third parties. During this Agreement and for 36 months after the final Bulk Order, the Client shall not bypass KarmaRise to solicit or place orders directly or indirectly with any Indian Manufacturing Partner introduced by KarmaRise, subject to the 25% liquidated damages provision of the Mutual NDA/NNN Agreement.

8. Liability Cap, Term & Governing Law

Aggregate liability under any Bulk Order shall not exceed 100% of the invoice value of that Bulk Order. This Agreement remains in force for an initial term of 2 years with automatic 1-year renewals unless terminated on 60 days' written notice. Governed by the laws of India, with exclusive jurisdiction in the courts of Thane and Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.
