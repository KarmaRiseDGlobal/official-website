Mutual Non-Disclosure, Intellectual Property Protection & Non-Circumvention Agreement

Document Ref: KRDG-NDA-2026-001 Effective Date: 16 November 2026

1. Parties

This Mutual Non-Disclosure, Intellectual Property Protection, and Non-Circumvention Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on the Effective Date above by and between:

  1. Karmarise Global LLP, a Limited Liability Partnership incorporated on 15 May 2025 under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 of India (LLPIN: ACO-3878, GSTIN: 27ABDFK8128R1ZL), having its registered office at 2nd floor, Guru Charan Apartment, Ulhasnagar, Maharashtra, India, 421002, operating the KarmaRise D Global brand ("KarmaRise"); and
  2. [Client Legal Entity Name], registered under the laws of [Spain / Country] with tax/registration ID [NIF / CIF / VAT Number] and registered address at [Client Registered Address] (the "Client").

2. Purpose

The Parties wish to evaluate, sample, and potentially execute a Manufacturing Programme under which KarmaRise develops and delivers custom or own-brand products for the Client through vetted Indian Manufacturing Partners (the "Purpose").

3. Confidential Information & Client IP Ownership

3.1 Client Confidential Information & IP: All product sketches, technical packs, garment or bag patterns, grading specifications, physical reference samples, material selections, brand assets, and Bulk Order forecasts disclosed by the Client remain the sole and exclusive intellectual property of the Client. Custom patterns, dies, or moulds created specifically for the Client under a Sampling Proposal vest in the Client upon payment of the agreed sampling fee.

3.2 KarmaRise Confidential Information: The identities, factory addresses, audit notes, capacity data, and pricing structures of KarmaRise's Indian Manufacturing Partners, tanneries, dye houses, and suppliers, together with KarmaRise's sampling proposals and costings, constitute confidential trade secrets of KarmaRise.

4. Non-Disclosure & Non-Use Obligations (NNN Pillar 1)

4.1 Strict Confidentiality: Each Party shall use the other Party's Confidential Information strictly for the Purpose. KarmaRise shall share the Client's specifications only with shortlisted Indian Manufacturing Partners on a strict need-to-know basis under written confidentiality obligations.

4.2 Zero Unauthorized Production: Neither KarmaRise nor any introduced Manufacturing Partner shall manufacture, display, sample, or sell any product derived from the Client's proprietary designs or patterns for any third party or for KarmaRise's own brands.

5. Non-Circumvention of Manufacturing Partners (NNN Pillar 2)

During the term of this Agreement and for thirty-six (36) months following the later of the last disclosure of Confidential Information or completion of the last Bulk Order, the Client shall not, directly or indirectly (including through any agent, affiliate, or third party), solicit, contact, or place orders directly with any Indian Manufacturing Partner or supplier introduced or disclosed by KarmaRise without KarmaRise's prior written consent. Any breach entitles KarmaRise to liquidated damages of 25% of the gross invoice value of such circumvented orders, in addition to injunctive relief.

6. Term, Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This Agreement remains in force for three (3) years from the Effective Date, with survival of trade secret and non-circumvention obligations for 36 months thereafter. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, with exclusive jurisdiction in the courts of Thane and Mumbai, Maharashtra, India, without prejudice to either Party's right to seek urgent injunctive relief in Spain, the European Union, or any competent court to prevent unauthorized disclosure or IP infringement.
