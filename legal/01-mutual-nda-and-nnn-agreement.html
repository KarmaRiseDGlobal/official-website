1. Parties

This Mutual Non-Disclosure, Intellectual Property Protection, and Non-Circumvention Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on the Effective Date above by and between:

  1. Karmarise Global LLP, a Limited Liability Partnership incorporated on 15 May 2025 under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 of India, bearing LLP Identification Number (LLPIN) ACO-3878 and Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) 27ABDFK8128R1ZL, having its registered office at 2nd floor, Guru Charan Apartment, Ulhasnagar, Maharashtra, India, 421002, operating under the brand KarmaRise D Global (hereinafter referred to as "KarmaRise", which expression shall include its Designated Partners, officers, and authorized representatives); and
  2. [Client Legal Entity Name], a company or business registered under the laws of [Country, e.g., Spain], bearing tax or registration number [NIF / CIF / VAT / Registration Number], having its registered office at [Client Registered Address] (hereinafter referred to as the "Client").

KarmaRise and the Client are each referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

2. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore, evaluate, and potentially execute a Manufacturing Programme under which KarmaRise develops, samples, coordinates, and delivers custom or own-brand products for the Client through vetted Indian Manufacturing Partners (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose proprietary technical, design, and commercial information to the other Party.

3. Definition of Confidential Information

3.1 Client Confidential Information: Includes all product sketches, technical packs (tech packs), garment, leather, accessory, or home decor patterns, grading tables, CAD files, physical reference samples, material and trim specifications, colourways, brand identity assets, target retail pricing, Bulk Order volume forecasts, and distribution plans shared by the Client with KarmaRise (whether through our website Product Brief form, video call, physical shipment, or email to @karmarisedglobal.com).

3.2 KarmaRise Confidential Information: Includes the identities, production site addresses, contact details, audit records, line capacities, pricing structures, and technical capabilities of KarmaRise's vetted Indian Manufacturing Partners, dye houses, tanneries, hardware suppliers, and logistics partners, as well as KarmaRise's sampling proposals, cost breakdowns, and quality control checklists.

3.3 Exclusions: Confidential Information does not include information that:

  • (a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement;
  • (b) was demonstrably in the receiving Party's lawful possession prior to disclosure;
  • (c) is independently developed without reference to the disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or
  • (d) is required to be disclosed by an order of a court or regulatory authority, provided prompt prior written notice is given to the disclosing Party where legally permitted.

4. Non-Disclosure and Permitted Use (NNN Pillar 1: Non-Disclosure & Non-Use)

4.1 Strict Confidentiality: Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall use it solely for the Purpose.

4.2 Controlled Partner Disclosure: KarmaRise shall disclose the Client's product specifications only to shortlisted Indian Manufacturing Partners on a strict need-to-know basis to evaluate feasibility, prepare sampling, or execute an approved Manufacturing Programme, ensuring such Manufacturing Partners are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement.

4.3 Zero Third-Party Commercialization (Including AlmaRise D Brand Carve-Out): Neither KarmaRise nor any Manufacturing Partner introduced by KarmaRise shall manufacture, reproduce, display in a showroom, sell, or supply any product derived from the Client's proprietary designs, patterns, moulds, or tech packs for any third party or for KarmaRise's own brands (including AlmaRise D Brand, AlmaRise D Reborn, AlmaRise D Organic, and AlmaRise D Home Decor) without the Client's express written consent.

5. Client Intellectual Property Ownership

5.1 Pre-Existing Intellectual Property: All pre-existing intellectual property, trademarks, sketches, patterns, and design rights provided by the Client remain the sole and exclusive property of the Client.

5.2 Custom Patterns and Tooling: Any custom patterns, cutting dies, moulds, or sample refinements developed specifically for the Client's product during a Manufacturing Programme shall vest in the Client upon payment of the agreed sampling or tooling fees set out in the applicable Product Brief & Sampling Proposal.

6. Mutual Non-Circumvention (NNN Pillar 2: Protection of Manufacturing Partner Network)

6.1 Vetted Partner Network: The Client acknowledges that KarmaRise invests substantial time, on-the-ground travel across India, and commercial expertise to identify, audit, and qualify Indian Manufacturing Partners.

6.2 Non-Circumvention Period: During the term of this Agreement and for a period of thirty-six (36) months following the later of (a) the last disclosure of Confidential Information or (b) the completion of the last Bulk Order between the Parties, the Client shall not, directly or indirectly (including through any affiliate, buying agent, or third-party intermediary), contact, solicit, place orders with, or bypass KarmaRise to deal directly with any Indian Manufacturing Partner, production unit, tannery, or supplier introduced or disclosed by KarmaRise without KarmaRise's prior written consent.

6.3 Remedy and Liquidated Damages: In the event of a breach of this Clause 6, KarmaRise shall be entitled to liquidated damages equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of the gross invoice value of all orders placed by or on behalf of the Client with the relevant Manufacturing Partner, or KarmaRise's standard programme margin, whichever is greater, without prejudice to injunctive relief.

7. Return or Destruction of Materials

Upon written request by the disclosing Party, the receiving Party shall promptly return or securely destroy all written Confidential Information (and, at the Client's expense and instruction, return or destroy any physical reference samples or prototypes), save for one archival copy retained strictly for legal and regulatory compliance.

8. Term

This Agreement comes into force on the Effective Date and remains in effect for three (3) years, and the obligations regarding Client trade secrets, custom patterns, and non-circumvention (Clause 6) shall survive for thirty-six (36) months after termination or expiration.

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. The competent courts in Thane and Mumbai, Maharashtra, India shall have exclusive jurisdiction, provided that either Party may seek interim injunctive or equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction (including in Spain or elsewhere in the European Union) to prevent unauthorized disclosure of Confidential Information or intellectual property infringement.
