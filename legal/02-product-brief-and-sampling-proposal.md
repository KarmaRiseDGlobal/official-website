## 3. Physical Swatches, Sampling Deliverables & Schedule (Stages 02 & 03 Scope)

1. **Physical Colour Swatch Approval (Stage 02):** KarmaRise guides material and dye specifications and prepares physical leather or fabric colour swatches for written Client approval before sample cutting begins. Colour is approved from physical swatches rather than phone photographs.
2. **Pattern Drafting & Material Sourcing:** KarmaRise translates the approved Product Brief into production-ready patterns and sources the agreed materials, trims, linings, antique brass hardware, and high-quality Japanese plastic YKK zippers in India.
3. **First Prototype (Proto 1):** Reviewed in person in India by KarmaRise and delivered within **[2 to 4] weeks** of receipt of the Sampling Fee and written design and swatch sign-off.
4. **Included Sample Revision Rounds:** Up to **[1 / 2] round(s)** of construction, fit, or detail adjustments resulting in the pre-production **Counter Sample (Golden Sample)** based on the Client's written feedback.
5. **Physical Sample Dispatch:** Physical samples are couriered via DHL / FedEx / UPS to the Client's nominated delivery address (**[Client Sample Delivery Address, Spain or elsewhere in the European Union]**).
