# Language and voice references

Canonical index for founder voice profiles, bilingual content standards, and `en-GB` / `es-ES` localization governance across KarmaRise D Global and AlmaRise D Brand.

## Founder voice profiles

- **Alma:** Read [ALMA.md](ALMA.md) before writing, editing, or translating any landing page copy, Story, newsletter, AlmaRise D Brand copy, social post, or outreach message in Alma's voice (especially Peninsular Spanish and English client, retail, design, or cultural writing).
- **Shirish:** Read [SHIRISH.md](SHIRISH.md) before writing, editing, or translating any Story, newsletter, social post, or outreach copy in Shirish's voice or about Shirish's role, legal work, NDA/contract structure, Indian craft hubs, Maharashtra heritage, or operational perspective.
